Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1 Assessment & Preferences

We start by understanding your loved one's specific care needs, daily routine, and personal preferences. This helps us match them with the most suitable caregiver who can provide personalized, compassionate support.

  • Create your family profile
  • Describe care needs in detail
  • Set your budget and schedule
  • Share special requirements or preferences

Step 2 Caregiver Selection

Browse through verified, background-checked caregivers who match your criteria. Each profile includes credentials, experience, specializations, and reviews from other families.

  • View detailed caregiver profiles
  • Read authentic family reviews
  • Check qualifications and certifications
  • Message caregivers directly

Step 3 Interview & Approval

Schedule a video or in-person meeting with your chosen caregiver. This is your opportunity to ask questions, ensure compatibility, and discuss care details before starting.

  • Schedule video interview
  • Discuss care expectations
  • Address any concerns
  • Finalize care plan

Step 4 Care Begins

Once you've approved, care starts on your preferred schedule. Our platform provides real-time updates, messaging, and emergency support 24/7.

  • Caregiver arrival for first visit
  • Initial care preferences review
  • Establish communication channels
  • Begin regular updates and check-ins

Step 5 Ongoing Management

We stay involved to ensure everything is working perfectly. Regular check-ins, adjustments, and support keep the care experience excellent for both families and caregivers.

  • Weekly care updates
  • Adjust schedule as needed
  • Access 24/7 support line
  • Rate and provide feedback

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to find a caregiver?

Typically, you can find and interview caregivers within 3-5 business days. We have an extensive network of verified professionals ready to help.

Are your caregivers truly verified?

Yes! Every caregiver undergoes thorough background checks, credential verification, and reference checks before joining our platform.

Can I change my caregiver if needed?

Absolutely. We want to ensure the best match. If you're not completely satisfied, we'll help you find an alternative caregiver.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and flexible payment plans. All payments are secure and encrypted.

Is there 24/7 customer support?

Yes! Our support team is available around the clock to help with emergencies, questions, or concerns.

How do you handle emergencies?

All caregivers are trained in emergency protocols. Our system alerts our support team immediately for rapid response and family notification.
